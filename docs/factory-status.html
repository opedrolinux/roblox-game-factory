The gap to a playable game

Four things stand between here and someone playing it

I can do these

Two, and one is half-done

  • 1 Finish the B2 persistence fixes Mid-flight. Until it lands, a server restart wipes every player's progress — and one confirmed bug lets a paid purchase vanish.
  • 2 Merge the world you can actually walk around The greybox islands, HUD and controls are built and reviewed, sitting unmerged on a side branch. Without them the game is logic with nothing to look at.
Only you can do these

Two, and they're the real gate

  • 3 Open the place in Studio once The T2 smoke test is written and waiting. It needs a human to open Roblox Studio — the fence forbids the agent from doing it. This is the single highest-value 20 minutes available.
  • 4 Publish it Publishing, and anything touching your account or real money, is permanently human-only.

Honest read: items 1 and 2 are a few hours of machine work. Item 3 is the one that could invalidate assumptions — until the place boots in-engine, "complete" means "complete under simulation", and this project has been burned by that difference before.
